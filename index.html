Born In BeiJing

When I was a child, I loved to wander through the alleys near my home, listening to the old people telling stories of the past and feeling the strong flavor of old Beijing. The food in Beijing is even more unforgettable to me, especially Beijing Roast Duck and Zhajiangmian. I remember once going to an old - fashioned restaurant with my family to have roast duck. The chef skillfully sliced the duck meat, and the aroma wafted through the air. Even thinking about it now still makes my mouth water. These wonderful memories make me deeply attached to the city of Beijing.
